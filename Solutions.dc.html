Solutions

One platform. Every kind of business.

youShop, youResto and youQR aren't just three products — they're building blocks. Combined with a few intelligent add-ons, every kind of merchant business gets a purpose-built solution on the same platform underneath.

01 · Hospitality

Digital Café

Table QR ordering, a self-service kiosk and a direct-order app end aggregator commission and payment friction.

  • ◆  QR at every table plus a kiosk — scan, order, pay and earn loyalty instantly.
  • ◆  A direct branded app saves 25–30% otherwise lost to aggregator commission.
  • ◆  AI demand forecasting and kitchen hygiene monitoring keep costs and compliance in check.
How it flows
Stock→Order→Kitchen→Pay→Book→Insights
02 · Distribution

FMCG Distribution

Route sales reps carry offline billing, live credit visibility and cashless collection into the field.

  • ◆  Offline billing syncs every order to HQ the moment signal returns.
  • ◆  Reps see each outlet's credit exposure before writing the invoice.
  • ◆  Pre-approved retailer lending, underwritten from purchase pattern — no paperwork.
How it flows
Stock→Route→Order→Pay→Dispatch→Book
03 · Mobility

Taxi & Fleet

In-cab payments, driver wallets and nightly automated settlement replace cash and spreadsheets.

  • ◆  Card, wallet and QR in-cab cut cash handling by more than 60%.
  • ◆  Automated nightly settlement — commission, bonus, fuel and tax withholding.
  • ◆  Lending against trip history for fuel and repair advances, auto-repaid from earnings.
How it flows
Request→Trip→Pay→Split→Wallet→Advance
04 · Manufacturing

Production Enterprise

One platform connects raw material, production costing, warehousing and every retail channel.

  • ◆  Recipe and BOM costing rolls material, labour and overhead into a live unit cost.
  • ◆  Inter-store transfers keep warehouse and outlet stock in sync, with no double-counting.
  • ◆  One settlement across counter, table QR, kiosk and online.
How it flows
Procure→Cost→Warehouse→Transfer→Sell→Close
05 · Ultra-SMB

Micro-merchant

Free forever software that grows into working-capital credit as a sales history builds.

  • ◆  Freemium is $0 forever, and runs on the merchant's own phone.
  • ◆  A live Predictability Index replaces paperwork as a credit score.
  • ◆  Pre-approved lending appears in the dashboard after about 90 days of sales.
How it flows
Install→Accept→History→Score→Loan→Repay
06 · Enterprise Retail

Multi-brand Chain

Central purchasing and consolidated books for franchise groups, with autonomy at the brand level.

  • ◆  One tenant, many brand storefronts, with centralised price and promo push.
  • ◆  Role-based access — franchisees see their own P&L, HQ sees everything.
  • ◆  Group books consolidate nightly, with VAT filed per entity.
How it flows
Procure→Distribute→Sell→Close→Consolidate→Insights
07 · Cloud Kitchen

Cloud Kitchen Operator

Many delivery brands run from one kitchen, unified onto a single order queue and settlement.

  • ◆  Every aggregator lands on one kitchen display — one screen, one queue.
  • ◆  Mark an item out of stock once; it disappears from every channel in seconds.
  • ◆  Per-brand settlement and demand forecasting to cut prep waste.
How it flows
Procure→Configure→Orders→Display→Settle→Forecast
