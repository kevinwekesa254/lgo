Cookie Policy

This policy explains how LGO uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, and how you can control them. It applies to visitors and merchants in Sri Lanka.

Last updated · July 2026 · Colombo, Sri Lanka

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. We also use similar technologies such as local storage — for example, to remember whether you prefer English, Sinhala, Tamil or Singlish so the site loads in your language next time.

2. Cookies we use

Essential cookies keep the site working — page navigation, security and session state; these cannot be switched off. Preference cookies remember your language and settings. Analytics cookies help us understand which pages merchants use so we can improve them. We do not use advertising cookies.

3. Third-party cookies

Some features rely on partners. When you reach a payment or banking step, our payment partner LankaPay and our local credit partner may set their own cookies, governed by their own policies. Embedded content, such as the Play Store link or WhatsApp chat, may also set cookies.

4. Managing cookies

You can accept or refuse non-essential cookies through your browser settings, and delete cookies already stored. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site working. Clearing your browser data will also reset your saved language preference.
